Roadmap to the platinum

“Vice Got a Price!” unlocks when the other 54 are done. Work the phases in order — each one feeds the next.

  1. Phase 1 — Play the story

    Chapters 1–4 and the final mission, plus the named story missions (Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory). This earns the 18 story trophies and opens the map. Stunts, vehicle discoveries and shop spending build up on the side — do not grind them yet.

  2. Phase 2 — Stranger missions

    Finish stranger strands across Leonida — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and the rest — until 8 strands are complete for “Strangers & Freaks” (silver). Strands are easy to leave half-done, so chase each stranger's marker until their final mission ends.

  3. Phase 3 — Activities & challenges

    Try every activity type once, then go for the wins: 20 different races, drone races at every location, gold medals at the shooting range, beating Cal at fishing, an arcade game, bowling at Kolt & Associates, 50 challenges and 250 stunts. Most of these are repeatable, so retry freely.

  4. Phase 4 — Collectibles

    Explore all of Leonida, meditate at every spot, WAiNK It at 100 locations, discover 100 vehicles and complete 3 collectible card sets. Do these on one big map sweep — they overlap heavily.

  5. Phase 5 — Cleanup & 100%

    Robberies and open-world feats (bank vaults, armored truck, gang operations, jewelry store), money goals ($250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, all garages, 10 Scooter Bros registrations), the partner trophies, and the three missable ones below. Finish with “A Long Road” — 100% completion — and the platinum pops.
